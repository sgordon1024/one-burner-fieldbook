Salsa chicken tacos

A jar of salsa does the seasoning. Beans stretch the chicken into two meals.

25 min · 2 servings

Ingredients

  • 12 oz, thawed and diced Frozen chicken breastWalmart
  • ¾ cup Medium salsaWalmart
  • A splash Reduced-sodium chicken brothWalmart
  • 1 can, drained No-salt-added black beansWalmart
  • 4 small tortillas Corn-wheat tortillasWalmart
  • ¼ cup, grated Sharp cheddarWalmart
  • 2 tbsp Plain Greek yogurtWalmart

Retailer links are options, not a live stock check.

Make it

  1. Simmer diced chicken in salsa with a splash of broth until the chicken reaches 165°F.
  2. Stir in black beans and warm through.
  3. Fill tortillas with chicken and beans. Top with cheddar and yogurt. Save half the filling for lunch.
