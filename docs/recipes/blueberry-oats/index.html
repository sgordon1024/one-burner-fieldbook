Blueberry peanut butter oats

A warm bowl from pantry staples and a handful of frozen berries.

10 min · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • ½ cup Old-fashioned oatsWalmart
  • ½ cup Frozen blueberriesWalmart
  • 1 tbsp Creamy peanut butterWalmart

Retailer links are options, not a live stock check.

Make it

  1. Simmer oats in 1 cup water, following the package directions.
  2. Stir in blueberries in the last minute and finish with peanut butter.
