Garlicky tuna penne

A pantry dinner for the end of the week, when the fresh stuff is running low.

20 min · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 4 oz dry Whole wheat penneWalmart
  • 1 can, with juices No-salt diced tomatoesWalmart
  • 1–2 tsp Minced garlicWalmart
  • 1 can, drained Chunk light tuna in waterWalmart
  • 1 tbsp Plain Greek yogurtWalmart
  • To taste Ground black pepperWalmart

Retailer links are options, not a live stock check.

Make it

  1. Simmer penne, diced tomatoes, garlic and about 1 cup water in one pot. Stir often and add water as needed.
  2. Once the pasta is tender, fold in the tuna and warm it through.
  3. Take off the heat, add yogurt and season with pepper.
