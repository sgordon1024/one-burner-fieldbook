Chicken & veggie rice

Brown rice, bite-size chicken and freezer vegetables in one covered pot.

55 min · 2 servings

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup dry Brown riceWalmart
  • As directed on the rice package Reduced-sodium chicken brothWalmart
  • 12 oz, thawed and diced Frozen chicken breastWalmart
  • 1½ cups Frozen mixed vegetablesWalmart

Retailer links are options, not a live stock check.

Make it

  1. Simmer the rice in broth according to the package’s liquid ratio.
  2. When the rice is nearly tender, stir in the chicken and vegetables. Cover and keep simmering, adding a splash of broth if needed.
  3. Cook until the rice is tender and the chicken reaches 165°F. Save the second serving for lunch.
