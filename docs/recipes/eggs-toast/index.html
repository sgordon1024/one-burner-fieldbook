Eggs & toast

Toast the bread in the skillet first and cook your eggs in the same pan.

10 min · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 1–2 slices Whole wheat breadWalmart
  • 2 Large eggsWalmart
  • 1 tsp Canola oilWalmart
  • Optional KetchupWalmart

Retailer links are options, not a live stock check.

Make it

  1. Toast bread in a dry skillet and transfer it to a plate.
  2. Add oil and scramble the eggs until set. Serve with toast and ketchup if you like.
