Tomato-spinach penne

The pasta cooks right in the sauce. No second pot or colander needed.

25 min · 2 servings

Ingredients

  • 6 oz dry Whole wheat penneWalmart
  • 1½ cups Pasta sauceWalmart
  • 2 handfuls Baby spinachWalmart
  • ¼ cup, grated Sharp cheddarWalmart

Retailer links are options, not a live stock check.

Make it

  1. Add the penne, sauce and 1½ cups water to a pot. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  2. Cook uncovered, stirring often and adding water a splash at a time until the pasta is tender.
  3. Fold in spinach until wilted and stir in cheddar. Save half for lunch.
